Features
- Set a daily water intake goal.
- Record water consumption in millilitres.
- View daily water intake.
- Calculate remaining water intake.
- Track daily goal completion.
- View weekly and monthly reports.
- Maintain water consumption history.
- Search records by date.
- Display average daily water consumption.
